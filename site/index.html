About

Min Jun Jeong (b. 2005, South Korea) is an emerging artist working in mixed media on canvas. His practice explores how invisible emotions and memories can be translated into layered visual structures. Through accumulations of color, texture, gesture, and light, he creates immersive surfaces where anxiety, stillness, and emotional resonance coexist.

Rather than depicting feeling directly, he reveals it through strata of material and rhythm, allowing memory to appear as something fragmented, refracted, and slowly reassembled.

His series Fragments of Memories, Layers of Emotion was exhibited at Modern Space Gallery, Korea (2025–2026). In 2025, he received an institutional award at the Busan International Kids & Youth Film Festival (BIKY). In 2026, he participated in the Seoul Art Fair as an emerging artist.
