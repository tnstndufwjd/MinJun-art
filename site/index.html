Selected Works2025 — 2026
01

Journey of Life

2026 · Oil on canvas · 60.6 × 72.7 cm

Journey of Life gathers fragments of memories shared with loved ones into a dense, interconnected field. Life and death, hope and despair, joy and sorrow coexist rather than resolve, echoing how memory holds contradictory emotions together. Through repeated blocks, botanical forms, and layered lines, Minjun turns these fragments into his distinctive resonance of memory – a visual map in which separation and loss are reconnected through remembrance, carrying the journey toward renewal.

02

Resonance in Memory

2026 · Mixed media on canvas · 72.7 × 60.6 cm

Resonance in Memory translates shifting emotional states into a tactile field of circles, color, and crossing lines. Bubble-like forms suggest a fragile lens through which the world is seen, while sand-like textures, translucent surfaces, and metallic lines create contrasting sensations of anxiety, safety, and joy. Rather than resolving these emotions, the painting allows them to coexist. Memory becomes a layered surface where tension and pleasure remain visible at the same time.

03

Entangled Wings

2026 · Mixed media on canvas · 53.0 × 45.5 cm

Entangled Wings captures the moment before release. Dense web-like lines press across the surface, while winglike forms appear to push outward against the surrounding structure. Dark segmented fields evoke pressure and confinement; luminous lines introduce movement, resistance, and the possibility of escape. The work does not depict freedom as an achieved state. Instead, it focuses on the effort of reaching toward it, turning anxiety and constraint into a physical, rhythmic struggle.

04

Object in Memory

2026 · Mixed media and acrylic on canvas · 45.5 × 53.0 cm

Object in Memory begins with the familiar presence of flowers and a vase, but gradually breaks the scene into irregular colored compartments. The object becomes less a still life than a structure for holding unstable feelings. Black outlines preserve traces of tension, while softer and brighter colors suggest comfort returning in fragments. The painting reflects a quiet attempt to rebuild emotional balance, using repetition, touch, and color to transform an ordinary object into a temporary place of safety.

05

Fragments of Stars

2026 · Mixed media and acrylic on canvas · 60.6 × 72.7 cm

Fragments of Stars grows from memories of winter evenings and Christmas markets in Switzerland. A green tree rises within a dark field of fractured lights, where small geometric shapes flicker like distant windows or stars. Joy and anxiety occupy the same space: warm colors gather around the tree, while the surrounding darkness remains present. The work treats memory as neither purely comforting nor painful, but as a layered experience in which warmth continues to shine through unease.

06

Éclat

2025 · Mixed media on canvas · 60.6 × 72.7 cm

Éclat focuses on the instant when a soap bubble bursts and light scatters across its surface. Metallic pigments, dark outlines, and compressed bands of color create a sense of fracture and radiance at once. The image hovers between abstraction and remembered sensation, turning a brief physical event into a study of perception. What disappears almost immediately is reconstructed through accumulated texture, suggesting that fragile moments can leave a surprisingly durable emotional trace.

About

Min Jun Jeong paints how the world feels rather than how it looks.

Min Jun Jeong (b. 2005, South Korea) is an emerging artist working in mixed media on canvas. His practice explores how invisible emotions and memories can be translated into layered visual structures. Through accumulations of color, texture, gesture, and light, he creates immersive surfaces where anxiety, stillness, and emotional resonance coexist.

Rather than depicting feeling directly, he reveals it through strata of material and rhythm, allowing memory to appear as something fragmented, refracted, and slowly reassembled.

His series Fragments of Memories, Layers of Emotion was presented in a solo exhibition at Gallery Artic, Seongnam (2025–26). In 2025, he received an institutional award at the Busan International Kids & Youth Art Fair (BIKAF). In 2026, he participated in the Seoul Art Fair as an emerging artist, and received the Next Generation Artist Award (차세대아티스트상) from the World Artist Interaction Association on October 12, 2026, for “Journey of Life” in the 2026 Seoul Visual Arts NEXT 100 – Young Artist Ecosystem Innovation Project.
